## Conflicts and deliberate adaptations

- No em dashes in new prose, but protected quotations, numerical en dashes, minus
  signs, and notation survive. Parentheses/colons/semicolons are allowed when useful.
- Watchlists are contextual. Valid technical terms, passive voice, genuine triples,
  first person, useful labels, and justified hedges are preserved.
- Quote typography and hyphenation follow actual grammar and house style, not
  blanket authorship-based prohibitions.
- Substantive coverage outranks exact paragraph counts; light edits remain conservative.
- Citation concentration is improved without silently deleting references.
- No invented evidence pointers, ranges, anecdotes, collaborators, or human persona.
- Formal proofs may prove; empirical verbs are calibrated, not categorically banned.
- Light editing flags scientific-position changes; substantive edits disclose them.
- Proposal templates do not assert current official requirements or reviewer behavior.
- Multiple drafts/reporting remain optional outputs; internal checks remain required.
- Host-specific invocation/tool metadata is not copied. Permissions stay with the host.
- Upstream examples are not copied wholesale; safe examples replace any example
  that adds unsupported factual detail. Deliberate messiness is not imported.

Coverage means every numbered rule family and academic layer has a destination
or explicit adaptation. It is a manual mapping, not a benchmark or semantic proof.
